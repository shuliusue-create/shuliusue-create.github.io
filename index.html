About Me

Welcome to my homepage! I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Geography at The University of Hong Kong. I earned my Ph.D. in atmospheric science from the Department of Earth System Science at Tsinghua University in 2024 and my B.S. in atmospheric science from the School of Atmospheric Science at Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology in 2019.

I study urban weather and climate and their interactions with air pollution and building energy use. I combine satellite and ground-based observations with climate–energy modelling at building, regional, and global scales and physics-informed machine learning. My research aims to support more sustainable cities by informing climate adaptation and building energy retrofits that reduce heat exposure, improve energy efficiency, and lower emissions of air pollutants and greenhouse gases.

Research Interests

Urban Weather and Climate

How does urban expansion affect air temperature, humidity, and rainfall in cities and surrounding regions?

Remote Sensing and Earth System Modelling

How can satellite-derived data on land cover and urban form improve weather and climate simulations at local, regional, and global scales?

Building Energy Use and Urban Climate Adaptation

How do urban climate and building heating and cooling demand influence each other, and which measures can reduce energy use and urban heat?

Hybrid Physics–AI Modelling of Urban Climate and Energy Use

How can physical models, satellite and ground-based observations, and machine learning be combined to map urban climate conditions and estimate building energy demand?

Environmental Impacts of Air Pollution Mitigation and Decarbonization

How do air pollution controls and decarbonization strategies affect climate, and how can these effects be managed to support clean-air and carbon-neutrality goals?

Climate Economics, Environmental Sustainability, and Equity

How do the economic impacts of climate change and the benefits of climate adaptation vary across places and population groups?

Research Methods
  • Satellite and in situ observations
  • Earth system modelling (CESM)
  • Regional weather modelling (WRF)
  • Land-surface modelling (CLM)
  • AI-enabled urban climate–energy mapping and modelling
  • Building energy modelling (EnergyPlus)
  • Land surface energy balance and temperature–humidity diagnostics
  • Integrated assessment of energy–water–land systems and climate policy (GCAM)
  • Climate–economy modelling (DICE, RICE)
